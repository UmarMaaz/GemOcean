Corundum · Red

Ruby

Ruby is corundum coloured by trace chromium — the same mineral family as sapphire, set apart by a red that stays vivid from daylight to candlelight.

A fine ruby is judged first on colour: a pure, slightly purplish red often described as ‘pigeon's blood’ commands the most attention, though warmer and cooler reds each have their own following. Clarity matters too, but rubies are rarely flawless — fine silk-like inclusions are common and, in modest amounts, don't diminish a stone's character.

Because untreated rubies of strong colour are genuinely scarce, most fine rubies on the market have been heat-treated to improve clarity or colour — a widely accepted practice we disclose plainly for every stone we offer.
