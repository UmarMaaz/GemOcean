Why We Started

A shorter distance between mine and collector

Gemocean began with a simple principle: too much of what's sold as a "natural gemstone" passes through intermediaries who never touch the mine, the cutter, or the stone itself. We wanted to close that distance.

Where We Source

Sourced in Pakistan, offered worldwide

Our stones come from Pakistan's own mining grounds — the corundum-rich mountains of the north and the emerald-bearing valleys that have supplied fine colour for generations. These are regions we know personally, not names on a certificate.

We buy from cutters and dealers we've built relationships with over years, not listings on a screen. From our base in Peshawar, we offer those stones directly to collectors and buyers around the world.

What We Value

Three things we won't compromise on

  • Honest Disclosure

    Treatments, origins and clarity characteristics are stated plainly — including the ones that make a stone uniquely natural.

  • Independent Certification

    We don't grade our own stock. Every gemstone is assessed by a recognised, independent gemological laboratory.

  • Unhurried Enquiry

    No countdown timers or checkout pressure. We discuss each stone directly via WhatsApp (+92 333 8021790) or email.

How We Work

From mine to your enquiry

A real sequence, not a marketing device — this is the order each stone actually moves through before it reaches you.

  • 01

    Sourcing

    Rough or cut stones are selected directly from trusted cutters and dealers across Pakistan.

  • 02

    Certification

    Each stone is sent to an independent gemological laboratory for species, treatment and quality assessment.

  • 03

    Curation

    We select which stones join the collection based on colour, character and honest condition — not just size.

  • 04

    Private Enquiry

    Interested buyers reach out directly. We discuss the stone, answer questions, and proceed only when it's a fit.
